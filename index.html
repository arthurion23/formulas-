Essential unit conversions

1 kHz = 10³ Hz · 1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz · 1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz

1 ms = 10⁻³ s · 1 μs = 10⁻⁶ s · 1 ns = 10⁻⁹ s

1 kbps = 10³ bps · 1 Mbps = 10⁶ bps · 1 mW = 10⁻³ W · 1 μW = 10⁻⁶ W

Remember: Shannon uses linear SNR. Nyquist bit-rate and Nyquist sampling are two different formulas. Use consistent units before substituting.
